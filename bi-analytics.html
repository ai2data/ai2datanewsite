What we build

Analytics that make the right action obvious.

We don’t hand you a dashboard and walk away. We engineer BI systems that surface what matters, predict what is next and tell your team exactly where to focus.

  • Executive dashboards

    Decision-ready views for leadership — not vanity metrics. Every chart answers a question; every number links to an action your team can take today.

  • KPI frameworks that align teams

    Most companies measure too much and understand too little. We define the handful of metrics that actually move the business, then wire them into every team’s workflow.

  • Self-service BI people actually use

    Your analysts shouldn’t be a bottleneck. Governed, intuitive platforms where any business user can answer their own questions in minutes — no SQL required.

  • Predictive & prescriptive analytics

    Stop reacting to last quarter. Models forecast churn, demand and revenue before they happen — then prescribe the actions to capture upside or cut losses.

  • Embedded analytics

    Intelligence where the work happens: live dashboards, alerts and drill-downs inside your product or internal tools — no tab-switching, no context loss.

  • Advanced & scenario analytics

    Cohort analysis, attribution modeling and what-if simulations that answer the hard questions a standard dashboard can never touch.

How we deliver

From messy data to clear decisions in four steps.

We start with the decisions, not the data.

  1. STEP 01

    Identify the decisions that matter

    We start with your highest-stakes business questions. KPIs are defined by what moves revenue, retention and margin.

  2. STEP 02

    Model for speed and trust

    Semantic layers and data marts built so every number is consistent, every query is fast and every user trusts what they see.

  3. STEP 03

    Build dashboards that prescribe action

    Interactive views designed around decisions, not decoration. Drill-downs lead to root causes; alerts fire before problems escalate.

  4. STEP 04

    Enable self-service across the org

    We train your people to explore, question and act on data themselves — so the analytics team stops being a ticket queue.
